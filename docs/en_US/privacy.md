# Privacy

This site is public technical documentation: no accounts, no comments, no paywall. Two
analytics services are used, for one purpose — learning which pages are read so the
documentation can be improved.

## What is collected

- the page path and the referring page;
- browser, device type and language;
- a coarse location derived from the IP address;
- in-site search terms;
- aggregate measures such as time on page and bounce rate.

The site does **not** ask for or collect names, email addresses, accounts or payment
details. Please do not put personal information in a URL parameter or in the search box.

## The services

| Service | Provider | Data location | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Google Analytics 4 | Google LLC | may be transferred outside China | traffic statistics, content improvement |
| Baidu Tongji (百度统计) | Baidu | within China | traffic statistics, content improvement |

Both set cookies (such as `_ga`, `_ga_*`, and the Baidu cookies). Browser settings, an ad
blocker or a private window can refuse them; reading is unaffected.

## Not used for

- advertising or cross-site profiling;
- selling data to third parties;
- identifying any individual.
